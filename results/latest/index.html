What the scenarios mean

Legitimate flash: varied source/timing signals. Attack flood: concentrated, regular traffic. Adaptive attack: attack traffic mimics the simple behavior features. Legitimate lookalike: real demand also resembles those features, exposing false positives.

Reproduce

From the repository root run PYTHONPATH=src python3 -m guardscale benchmark --seeds 5 --out results/latest. Full per-run outputs and the assumptions are included beside this page.
